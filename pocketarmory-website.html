// What it does

Everything Your
Collection Needs

Built for responsible owners who want to stay organized without signing up for anything or putting their data anywhere but their own device.

Firearm Inventory

Track make, model, caliber, serial number, purchase details, and photos. Store proof of purchase with automatic timestamps. Every firearm documented for insurance or peace of mind.

Cleaning Tracker

Set a round count threshold per firearm. Every logged session automatically increments the counter. Get notified when it's time to clean. Full cleaning history with products used.

Ammo Inventory

Track calibers, brands, grain weights, quantities, and storage locations. Rounds auto-deduct when you log a session. Low stock alerts when you're running low on a caliber.

Session Logging

Log shooting sessions with date, location, and round count. Automatically updates firearm maintenance counters and deducts from ammo inventory. Full history per firearm.

Accessories

Track optics, lights, suppressors, and grips attached to each firearm. Document serial numbers, purchase info, and proof of purchase for every accessory you own.

Private by Design

No accounts, no servers, no cloud you don't control. All data lives on your device in a local SQLite database. Google Drive backup to your own storage coming soon.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 26, 2026  ·  Last Updated: March 26, 2026
Short version: PocketArmory does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Everything stays on your device. We don't know who you are, what firearms you own, or how you use the app.
1. Introduction

PocketArmory ("the App", "we", "our") is a personal firearm inventory, maintenance tracking, and ammo management application developed for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

By using PocketArmory, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

PocketArmory does not require an account, registration, or any personal information to use. All data you enter into the App — including firearm details, serial numbers, ammo inventory, session logs, and photos — is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database.

We do not collect, transmit, receive, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address, phone number)
  • Firearm inventory data, serial numbers, or purchase information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to external servers
  • Photos or documents you attach to firearm or accessory records
3. How Your Data is Stored

All data entered into PocketArmory is stored locally on your Android device in a private SQLite database. This database is only accessible to the App and is not shared with any third parties.

Photos and documents you attach to firearm or accessory records are stored in the App's private storage directory on your device. These files are not accessible to other apps without your permission.

If you delete the App, all associated data — including your entire inventory database and any attached photos — will be permanently deleted from your device.

4. Internet Access and Network Communication

PocketArmory does not require an internet connection to function. The App operates entirely offline.

The App does not make any network requests, send data to any servers, or communicate with any external services. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party data collection tools integrated into the App.

A future optional feature — Google Drive backup — will allow you to back up your data to your own personal Google Drive account. This feature will be opt-in, clearly disclosed, and will only transmit data to your own Google account using Google's official APIs. We will never have access to your backup data. This feature is not available in the current version of the App.

5. Permissions

PocketArmory may request the following Android permissions:

  • Camera / Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — Used solely to allow you to take photos of your firearms and accessories, or attach existing photos from your gallery. Photos are stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — Used to deliver local cleaning reminders and low ammo alerts. These notifications are generated entirely on-device and do not involve any external servers or services.
  • Receive Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — Used to reschedule local notification reminders after your device restarts.

No permissions are used for any data collection or transmission purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

PocketArmory does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect user data. Specifically, the App does not include:

  • Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any analytics platform
  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Crash reporting services (e.g. Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • Social login or OAuth integrations
  • Any other third-party data collection tools
7. Children's Privacy

PocketArmory is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can address the situation.

8. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted externally, the security of your data is primarily protected by your device's built-in security features (screen lock, device encryption, etc.).

We recommend enabling device encryption and using a strong screen lock to protect the sensitive information stored in the App.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you use the App. You have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can delete individual records (firearms, ammo entries, accessories, sessions, cleaning logs) at any time from within the App
  • You can erase all data via the "Erase All Data" option in Settings (this action is permanent and cannot be undone)
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all associated data from your device
10. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any of your personal data on our servers, traditional data subject rights (access, portability, erasure) apply directly to your device rather than to us. You maintain complete control and ownership of all data in the App at all times.

If you have concerns about data associated with the App's listing on Google Play (such as your Google account's purchase history), please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes — such as introducing a new feature that involves data collection — we will update this page and revise the "Last Updated" date at the top.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Google Play Requirements

This Privacy Policy is provided to comply with Google Play's requirements for apps listed on the Google Play Store. PocketArmory is categorized as an app that does not collect, share, or handle personal or sensitive user data as defined by Google Play's Data Safety guidelines.

In Google Play's Data Safety section for PocketArmory, we declare:

  • No data is collected from users
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • No data is transmitted outside of the user's device
  • All data handling is optional and user-initiated
13. Contact Us
Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how PocketArmory handles data, please contact us. We'll do our best to respond promptly.
